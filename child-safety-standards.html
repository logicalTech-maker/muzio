Child Safety Standards

At Muzio, we are committed to providing a safe environment for all users. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) or any content that exploits, endangers, or harms children.

Our Commitment

Muzio strictly prohibits:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Any content that sexually exploits or endangers minors.
  • Solicitation, grooming, or predatory behavior involving children.
  • Sharing or promoting abusive or exploitative content involving minors.

Reporting

Users can report inappropriate posts or content directly within the app. Reports are reviewed promptly, and content that violates our policies may be removed. Accounts involved in serious violations may be permanently suspended.

Enforcement

We reserve the right to remove any content or suspend accounts that violate these standards. Where required by applicable law, we may cooperate with relevant law enforcement authorities.
